⚠️ Time-Sensitive Information

Burnaby R1 — October 2025 Amendments
In October 2025 Council reduced what the R1 SSMUH district allows: maximum height dropped from 4 storeys to 3 (10 m), rear principal buildings are limited to 2 storeys, lot coverage and achievable floor area were cut, and parking minimums were raised outside provincially-designated transit-oriented areas. The buildable envelope is now tighter than earlier 2024 guidance — design must be confirmed against Burnaby's current R1 standards in Phase 2.
Federal GST Rental Rebate
build generally before 2031
The federal government's 100% rebate of the 5% GST on new purpose-built rental housing (projects of 4+ units, 90%+ long-term rental) applies in BC. Construction must generally begin before 2031. Structuring the project as qualifying rental from Day 1 is essential to capture this — confirm eligibility in Phase 2.
Parking & CMHC
Provincial rules limit Burnaby's ability to require parking on R1 lots inside transit-oriented areas (roughly 57% of R1 lots). CMHC policy can change at any time and affects financing — applying early reduces risk.

4. Development Options

Option A — Triplex / Fourplex (3–4 units, as-of-right)
A triplex or fourplex built directly on the lot — the baseline R1 SSMUH entitlement on a serviced Burnaby residential lot, with no rezoning or public hearing. Lots under 280 m² are limited to 3 units; lots of 280 m² or more allow 4. Buildable size is governed by the October 2025 R1 standards — 3-storey / 10 m height, reduced lot coverage and floor area, and setbacks — confirmed in Phase 2.
Option B — Six-Unit Multiplex (near frequent transit) — Primary Recommendation
Where the lot is larger than 280 m² and within roughly 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, Burnaby's R1 District permits up to six units as-of-right — the highest-density, strongest-income direction without rezoning. Confirming the lot's transit proximity and area is the first gating step, since it is what unlocks the 6-unit tier. Inside a transit-oriented area, minimum parking cannot be required. Confirmed in Phase 2.
Option C — Suites Route (secondary + laneway suite)
A lower-complexity path: keep the principal dwelling and add a secondary suite plus a detached laneway (coach) house — Burnaby's R1 District permits both on the same lot, and each counts toward the unit total. This is often the fastest route to rental income while a larger multiplex is evaluated. Suite sizes and siting confirmed in Phase 2.
