2. Current Zoning

Current ZoningResidential base zone under City of Port Moody Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 (2018). Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) is now permitted as-of-right on eligible residential lots following the City's Bill 44 zoning updates (completed June 25, 2024). The exact base zone is to be confirmed.
Provincial FrameworkBritish Columbia's Housing Statutes (Residential Development) Amendment Act, 2023 (Bill 44) requires municipalities to permit small-scale multi-unit housing as-of-right on most single-family and duplex lots. Port Moody has implemented this through amendments to Zoning Bylaw No. 2937, with further amendments in 2025 (Bylaw No. 3506) extending SSMUH into the City's CD zones.
As-of-Right Unit RangeUp to 3 units on lots under 280 m² (3,014 sq ft); up to 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m² (1 acre); and up to 6 units where the lot is within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop (service every 15 minutes or better), with minimum parking eliminated. Lots over 1 acre are exempt from the SSMUH requirement — confirm this lot's area.
Permitted UsesMulti-unit housing forms — the SSMUH provisions allow multiple self-contained dwelling units on an eligible residential lot without rezoning, subject to technical review of site conditions and Port Moody's SSMUH design guidelines.
Development Goals Achievable?YES — a multiplex is enabled as-of-right (at minimum 3–4 units, up to 6 near frequent transit); proceed to Step 2 — Builder Ready Package™

4. Development Options

Option A — 3–4 Unit Multiplex (As-of-Right SSMUH)
A small-scale multiplex of three units (on lots under 280 m²) or four units (on lots between 280 m² and 1 acre), permitted as-of-right under Port Moody's SSMUH provisions. No rezoning or public hearing is required. The exact unit count is set by the confirmed lot area. Building form, setbacks, height, and site coverage follow the SSMUH standards in Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 and the City's SSMUH design guidelines. This tier is the reliable floor for the multiplex goal regardless of transit proximity.
Option B — Up to 6-Unit Multiplex (Near Frequent Transit) — Primary Recommendation if Eligible
Where the lot is within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop (service every 15 minutes or better), the SSMUH rules permit up to six units and eliminate the minimum-parking requirement. This is the configuration that best matches the goal of maximizing unit count. Eligibility hinges on the confirmed transit distance — a defined, checkable input. If it clears, six units proceed as-of-right on the same no-rezoning path as Option A.
Option C — Confirming the Two Inputs That Set the Ceiling
Two site facts determine the maximum unit count: the confirmed lot area (3 units under 280 m²; 4 units from 280 m² to 1 acre; lots over 1 acre are exempt from SSMUH) and whether the lot is within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop (which unlocks the six-unit tier and removes parking minimums). Confirming both — via a survey / BC Assessment lot record and the City's transit-proximity mapping — is the essential first step, and is completed during the Builder Ready Package™ feasibility phase.

How you finance the development of your property can significantly impact your overall ROI, as well as your cashflow and stability.

Many property owners destroy their project margins by trying to use expensive personal cash lines or basic retail bank loans.

A successful project requires institutional underwriting before you ever pull a permit. We partner with national commercial lending desks to bypass conventional retail hurdles, allowing our qualified files to access specialized programs, rates, and terms completely unavailable to the general public.

Mortgage RefinanceIf a property has appreciated in value or the mortgage has been significantly paid down, owners can typically access up to 80% of the property's newly appraised value.
Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)Is a flexible, revolving line of credit secured against the equity of an existing property. Unlike a refinance that issues a lump sum, a HELOC functions more like a high-limit credit card tied to your real estate.
Construction FinancingAlso known as a progress-draw mortgage, construction financing is a short-term loan specifically designed to fund a new build from the ground up (or fund a massive structural overhaul).
CMHC MLI Select (available at 5+ rental units)CMHC's flagship multi-unit insured-financing product offers preferred terms — higher loan-to-value and longer amortization — for qualifying purpose-built rental projects. It requires a minimum of five rental units, so it attaches to the six-unit near-transit tier. (Source: CMHC MLI Select.)
CMHC Apartment Construction Loan Program (minimum $1M loan)Low-cost construction financing for purpose-built rental. It is gated on loan size — a minimum $1M loan — rather than unit count, so eligibility depends on the confirmed Phase 2 project budget. (Source: CMHC ACLP.)
