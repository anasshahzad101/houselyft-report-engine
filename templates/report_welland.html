4. Development Options

Option A — Three-Unit Multiplex / Triplex (your goal)
A purpose-built three-storey building with one self-contained three-bedroom unit per level — the triplex you described. Up to three residential units are permitted as-of-right on a serviced low- or medium-density residential lot under Ontario's Bill 23 and Welland By-law 2024-99 (in force October 1, 2024); no rezoning is contemplated. The build must fit Welland's residential built-form standards — setbacks, height, and lot coverage — confirmed against the parcel's zone in Phase 2. The additional residential units are exempt from development charges under Bill 23.
Option B — Three Units via Dwelling + Two Additional Units
The same three-unit envelope delivered as a main dwelling plus up to two additional residential units — for example an interior suite and a detached rear unit. Welland permits up to two additional units in association with a single-detached, semi-detached, two-unit or townhouse dwelling under By-law 2024-99 §5.2, where no other ancillary building on the lot already contains a unit. This route can suit a phased build or a retained existing home. Unit sizes and siting are confirmed in Phase 2.
Option C — Scaling Beyond Three Units (Upside)
Three units is the as-of-right envelope on a standard serviced residential lot. A larger purpose-built rental — four or more units — is a different pathway: it depends on the parcel's specific zone permissions (and may involve a zoning by-law amendment), but it also unlocks additional programs. At four or more self-contained rental units the federal GST/HST Purpose-Built Rental Housing rebate becomes available, and at five or more units CMHC's MLI Select financing framework opens up. Whether four-plus units is feasible on this lot is confirmed in Phase 2.

How you finance the development of your property can significantly impact your overall ROI, as well as your cashflow and stability.

A successful project requires institutional underwriting before you ever pull a permit. We partner with national commercial lending desks to bypass conventional retail hurdles, allowing our qualified files to access specialized programs, rates, and terms completely unavailable to the general public.

Mortgage RefinanceIf a property has appreciated in value or the mortgage has been significantly paid down, owners can typically access up to 80% of the property's newly appraised value.
Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)Is a flexible, revolving line of credit secured against the equity of an existing property. Unlike a refinance that issues a lump sum, a HELOC functions more like a high-limit credit card tied to your real estate.
Construction FinancingAlso known as a progress-draw mortgage, construction financing is a short-term loan specifically designed to fund a new build from the ground up (or fund a massive structural overhaul).
